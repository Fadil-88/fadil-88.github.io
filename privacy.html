Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who this covers

Rotation Tracker is a personal app used only by its owner, Fadil Ahmed. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): booking emails from a short list of approved travel senders, to read flight, hotel and taxi details.
  • Google Drive (read-only): one folder ("Offshore schedule tracker widget") holding the owner's schedule sheet, tickets and booking vouchers.

The app cannot send, change or delete any email or file.

How the data is used and stored

Only the travel facts needed for the widget (flight numbers, times, hotel names and dates, taxi bookings) are saved, in the owner's private GitHub repository. Email bodies and documents are not stored. Document text is sent to the Anthropic API solely to extract those travel facts.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Rotation Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
